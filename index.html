Frequently Asked Questions

What is the advantage of running dual compressors over a single compressor?
Running dual compressors cuts your tank fill and recovery times in half. Furthermore, because each compressor runs for half the time to fill the tank, they generate far less internal thermal heat, preserving the internal PTFE piston rings and dramatically extending compressor lifespan.
Why is 3/8-inch airline preferred over 1/4-inch or 1/2-inch for street air ride?
**3/8-inch airline** is considered the universal sweet spot for air suspension. 1/4" line rises slowly and deliberately (great for precision track adjustments), while 1/2" line slams violently up and down in under a second. 3/8" provides a fast, crisp 2.5-second lift that is responsive without spilling your coffee.
Why is an automated water trap mandatory on air suspension tanks?
Compressing atmospheric air concentrates atmospheric humidity, which condenses into liquid water inside the aluminum air tank. Without an inline water trap filter, that water travels into electronic solenoid valve manifolds, causing sticky valves and freezing solid in winter temperatures, locking the car at slammed height.
